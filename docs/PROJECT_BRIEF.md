# Web2Go project brief

## What Web2Go does

Web2Go is a premium European technology studio. It helps businesses become
visible, understandable, verifiable, and easier to cite in AI-generated
answers from systems such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity.

Web2Go combines website strategy, information architecture, technical SEO,
semantic HTML, structured data, authority content, and conversion design. The
work treats a website as both a human experience and a machine-readable source
of evidence.

## Audience

### Primary

- Founders and senior leaders of European B2B companies.
- Marketing, communications, and digital leaders responsible for discoverability.
- Professional-service firms whose expertise must be explained and verified.
- Growth-stage businesses planning a redesign or authority-building programme.

### Secondary

- SEO and content teams that need a measurable AI visibility baseline.
- Product and engineering teams improving rendering, data, and site structure.
- Agencies seeking a specialist partner for semantic and technical delivery.

The audience is commercially experienced and skeptical of vague AI claims. The
site must explain the method plainly and show evidence of rigor without
pretending that proof exists where it does not.

## Positioning

Web2Go is not a generic SEO agency, SaaS product, or AI consultancy. It is a
technically sophisticated studio that designs the source systems from which
search engines and AI assistants learn about a business.

The brand is premium, European, editorial, restrained, cinematic,
contemporary, and confident. It communicates precision through structure,
typography, pacing, and evidence rather than decoration or hype.

## Value proposition

Web2Go builds and optimizes websites so AI systems can understand, trust, and
cite a business. Clients receive clearer information architecture, stronger
entity signals, traceable evidence, answer-ready content, and a better human
path from discovery to enquiry.

## Services

1. **AI Visibility Audits** — inspect discovery, entity clarity, evidence, and
   citation readiness; return an evidenced baseline and prioritized actions.
2. **AI-ready Website Design and Development** — create fast, accessible,
   semantically structured sites that communicate to people and machines.
3. **Semantic and Technical Optimization** — improve rendering, crawl paths,
   metadata, internal relationships, schema, and source consistency.
4. **Authority and Content Systems** — design repeatable structures for expert
   content, authorship, provenance, updates, and supporting evidence.
5. **Information Architecture and Technical SEO** — organize entities,
   services, locations, topics, and navigation into a coherent system.
6. **Landing Pages and Conversion Optimization** — turn qualified discovery
   into a clear next action without sacrificing evidence or accessibility.

## Brand personality

- **Precise:** every claim has a reason and every relationship is deliberate.
- **Calm:** confidence comes from clarity, not volume.
- **Investigative:** the studio finds gaps between what a company knows and
  what machines can verify.
- **Editorial:** information is sequenced, paced, and given hierarchy.
- **Technical:** implementation details are real, but explained in plain language.
- **Honest:** no guaranteed rankings, citations, fabricated proof, or vanity scores.

## Core narrative

The homepage follows this progression:

`Question → fragmented information → structured context → verification → citation → answer`

The opening line, **Be / the / answer**, states the outcome. The information
landscape begins scattered, then resolves as the user scrolls. Web2Go's method
is introduced as the mechanism that turns disconnected website material into a
source an AI system can discover, understand, verify, and cite.

## MVP launch scope

- One English homepage with the approved fourteen-section structure.
- Responsive navigation and contact CTA.
- Original information-landscape art direction.
- Four service presentations, methodology, citation explanation, process, and
  honest capability demonstrations.
- Semantic HTML, metadata, structured-data foundations, accessibility, and
  performance controls.
- Two purposeful desktop scroll narratives with reduced-motion and mobile alternatives.

The MVP excludes a CMS, forms, authentication, analytics, case-study claims,
multilingual routes, and detailed service pages until content and operational
requirements are approved.
