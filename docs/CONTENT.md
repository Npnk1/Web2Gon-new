## 5. AI visibility methodology

**Label:** A measurable methodology

**H2:** We measure observable signals, not vanity scores.

**Intro:**

An AI visibility audit records what can be discovered, how clearly it can be
interpreted, where evidence is available, and whether a useful answer has a
source worth citing. Every finding links to an observed page, response or
technical signal.

### Discovery coverage

**Question:** Can important information be reached?

**Measure:** Indexable pages, rendering, crawl paths, canonical signals,
internal links and machine access.

**Output:** An evidenced map of visible, blocked, duplicated and missing sources.

### Entity clarity

**Question:** Can systems identify the business and its relationships?

**Measure:** Organization, service, expertise, location, author and topic
consistency across content, markup and trusted profiles.

**Output:** An entity map with ambiguous, conflicting and unsupported relationships.

### Evidence density

**Question:** Can important claims be checked?

**Measure:** Named sources, dates, authors, first-party facts, supporting pages,
method descriptions and provenance.

**Output:** A claim-to-source register with evidence gaps and priority actions.

### Citation readiness

**Question:** Is there a clear passage worth citing?

**Measure:** Answerable language, page specificity, source authority, update
signals, semantic structure and traceable context.

**Output:** A set of citation opportunities with the exact source pages that
need to be created or improved.

**Action:** See what an audit includes

## 10. How AI discovers and cites a company

**Label:** The answer path

**H2:** A citation is the end of a chain.

**Intro:**

No optimization can guarantee that an AI system will cite a company. But the
source can make each step clearer, more consistent and easier to verify.

### Question

A buyer asks for an explanation, comparison, provider or recommendation.

### Discovery

Search and retrieval systems locate pages that appear relevant and accessible.

### Extraction

The systems identify passages, facts and relationships that may answer the question.

### Entity resolution

Names, services, people, locations and topics are connected to the right organization.

### Verification

Claims are compared with supporting pages, sources, dates, authors and other signals.

### Citation

A clear, relevant and trustworthy source may be selected to support the generated answer.
